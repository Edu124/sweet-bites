Sweet Bites

Your menu and your prices, taking orders on WhatsApp all day. Customers pick a cake, choose the size, add a message, pay by UPI and get updates, without you typing a single reply.

  • 🧁The menu, the WhatsApp way. A tap-to-choose list in the chat, and a photo menu one link away.
  • ✍️Cake messages and dates. "Happy Birthday Aarav", delivery date, time slot, pickup or delivery.
  • 💳UPI payment in the chat. The order is confirmed only after the money arrives.
  • 📲Every order on one screen. No missed chats at midnight, and order updates go out to the customer automatically.

Try it on the phone: tap the buttons and links, or type things like biscoff, menu, nutella or cart.
