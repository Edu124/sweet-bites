Summary

Sweet Bites pays ₹9,999 once to go live, then only for the messages sent: ₹0.35 for each order reply or order update and ₹1.20 for each offer message. There is no monthly fee. At about 300 orders a month that comes to roughly ₹2,900 a month, or under ₹10 per order.

Selly is a registered WhatsApp Tech Provider with Meta. Sweet Bites' number is connected to the official WhatsApp Business Platform directly through Selly, with no other reseller in between. The number and the customer list stay Sweet Bites' own.

What customers get is the ordering assistant in the Sweet Bites demo: the menu as a tap-to-choose list, a photo menu link, sizes and prices, a message on the cake, date and time slot, delivery or pickup, UPI payment in the chat, and automatic order updates.

One-time setup: ₹9,999
